Lawyering

A workspace for law firms and individual practitioners: cases, engagements, documents, billing and client communication in one place.

CTO, building solo with AI agents · 2026 – present · lawyering.cloud ↗

Business goal

Turn the paperwork of legal practice into a digital workspace. The founder, a lawyer, wanted a Slack for law firms, where firms manage their cases, engagements, documents and clients in one secure place.

Context

  • –Two kinds of tenant: law firms with staff, roles and departments, and individual practitioners. Clients are invited by a firm and get their own portal with their cases, engagements and invoices.
  • –Lawyers need to share documents with clients and outside parties who will never sign up for an account.
  • –One engineer. I build it agent-first, under rules enforced by agent-contract.

Decisions

A modular monolith

Each domain is a self-contained Laravel module: Cases, Engagements, Drive, Chat, PayTrack, Signatures, StaffTrack and more. Modules talk only through services and events, which keeps boundaries clear for me and for the agents writing code inside them.

Tenant scoping by default

Every tenant-owned record carries a tenant ID. Middleware resolves the firm or practitioner from the route, and a global scope filters every query, so cross-tenant leaks need a deliberate bypass rather than a forgotten where-clause.

Encrypted at rest, not end-to-end, for Drive

Inline previews, ZIP extraction and viewers without accounts all need the server to read the file. So Drive encrypts files at rest on the server, and access is controlled by participants, expiring share links and optional access codes.

Share links that work without an account

A link can expire after 24 hours, 7 days, 30 days or never, can require an access code, and locks for 10 minutes after repeated wrong codes, notifying the folder's creator.

Billing that matches how firms get paid

PayTrack has no payment gateway. A billing record holds one period, renewals create the next one, and invoices are in naira or US dollars, with reports that never add the two together.

Result

  • –In production since June 2026, with Cases and Engagements, Drive, PayTrack billing, StaffTrack, e-signatures, and Google Calendar and Google Drive integration.
  • –The latest release, in October 2026, added automatic retainer renewals and billing in naira or US dollars.
  • –19 modules and over 1,200 automated tests, built by one engineer working with agents since May 2026.
