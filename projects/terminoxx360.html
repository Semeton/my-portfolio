Terminoxx360

Real-time ATM fleet monitoring, running at Access Bank.

Maintainer at Access Bank; sole engineer on the new version · cloud.terminoxx360.com ↗

Business goal

Give a bank's operations team a reliable live view of its ATM fleet, and let one deployment serve a banking group's subsidiaries across several countries.

Context

  • –The version running at Access Bank had transaction-heavy jobs that stalled under database locks, plus a persistent backlog of slow queries.
  • –Each new country or subsidiary would otherwise have needed its own deployment.

Decisions

Fix the running system first

Rewrote the stalling jobs for transactional efficiency, added index-based query optimisation and schema clean-up, and added auto-restart for stuck jobs.

Rebuild around the locking problem

Designed the new version so the jobs that used to contend for locks no longer do.

Tenancy by country and subsidiary

One deployment serves multiple countries and regional subsidiaries, with full brand customisation per tenant.

Model provider as configuration

AI-generated overviews, reports and alerts can run on Ollama, Claude or OpenAI.

Result

  • –Cleared the slow-query backlog and the stalled jobs at Access Bank.
  • –A new multi-tenant version with map views, full monitoring history and reporting behind a deliberately simpler UI.
