Terminoxx360

ATM fleet monitoring and incident response, running at Access Bank and rebuilt for multi-country banking groups.

Maintainer at Access Bank; sole engineer on the new version · cloud.terminoxx360.com ↗

Business goal

Tell a bank which ATMs are down before its customers do, get the right person fixing each one, and report uptime the way the Central Bank of Nigeria requires. The new version serves a banking group's subsidiaries across several countries from one deployment.

Context

  • –At Access Bank, transaction-heavy jobs stalled under database locks, and a backlog of slow queries kept growing.
  • –The banks' ATM middleware has no webhooks, so status has to be polled.
  • –Different people need different uptime numbers. CBN reports use a regulatory formula that weights downtime ten times; operations need uptime based on missed SLAs.
  • –Every bank signs in differently: local accounts, LDAP and Active Directory, or the bank's own API. Some cannot send any data outside their network.

Decisions

Fix the running system first

At Access Bank I rewrote the stalling jobs for transactional efficiency, added indexes and schema clean-up to clear the slow queries, and added auto-restart for stuck jobs.

One job owns every ticket

Every minute a single job closes tickets for recovered ATMs, escalates overdue ones from custodian to support to vendor, and opens tickets for ATMs offline past tolerance in business hours. It never creates duplicates across workers, pauses itself and alerts admins when the feed goes stale, and stops the SLA clock on holidays.

Two uptime numbers, never mixed

A pure calculator with no database access produces the regulatory and internal figures separately, pre-computed for 12, 24, 48 and 72-hour windows, so compliance reports and operations dashboards each get the number they need.

Tenancy by subsidiary

Every table carries a subsidiary ID enforced by the database and a global query scope. Roles are scoped per subsidiary, so an admin in one is not an admin in another, and a subsidiary can inherit settings from its parent.

Configuration, not forks, for each bank

Sign-in adapters for LDAP and bank APIs plug in per deployment. The AI assistant runs on Claude where the bank allows cloud access, or on Ollama inside the bank's network where no data may leave.

Result

  • –Cleared the slow-query backlog and the stalled jobs at Access Bank.
  • –A multi-tenant rebuild, started in April 2026, with map views, full monitoring history, reports and an AI assistant that queries live fleet data, covered by over 600 automated tests.
